Pin Frame – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Pin Frame is a personal app used only by its owner. It reads the owner's own Pinterest boards and pins through the Pinterest API (read-only) and stores them locally on the owner's computer.

No data is collected from other people. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not create, edit or delete any Pinterest content.

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings. Locally stored data can be deleted by removing the app's data folder.
